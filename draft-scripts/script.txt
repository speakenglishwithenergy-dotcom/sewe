## 🕒 Part 2 – Why English?

So, why did I choose English?

1. First, as a developer, English is Everywhere.
From coding to documentation, all are in English.

2. Second, the global tech community uses English.
Developers from different countries
communicate, learn, and help each other in English.

3. Third, English opens up better opportunities.
Right now, AI is moving incredibly fast. At the same time, layoffs are happening at many big tech companies.

That made me think:
"What if I lose my job one day?"
"How do I stay competitive?"
"How do I keep growing instead of falling behind?"

I realized that improving my technical skills isn't enough. English is just as important.

It helps me understand the best learning resources, read documentation faster, communicate with developers around the world, and apply for opportunities that simply aren't available if I only speak my native language.

Yes, the competition becomes much bigger.
But so do the opportunities.

4. And finally... Speaking English gives me confidence.

It helps me express my ideas more clearly, work with people from different countries, and become the kind of developer who can contribute anywhere in the world.

That's the developer I want to become.

---

## 🕒 Part 3 – This Channel Is My Practice



This channel is more than a place where I share programming knowledge, tell stories, or talk about life as a developer.

It's also my personal practice.

Over the years, I've tried many different ways to improve my English. I watched videos, I took courses, I used language learning apps. Some of them helped, but I still don't feel that my English has improved as much as I hoped.

Eventually, I realized that the only way to get better at English is through regular practice. Not for a few days or weeks, but continuously over a long period of time.

However, the one big challenge is, how to staying motivated. How do you practice every day without getting bored or giving up?

At the same time, I'd always wanted to start a YouTube channel. I spent years thinking about it, but I never found a topic that I truly connected with—or the confidence to finally get started.

Then I realized that I could combine several things I genuinely enjoy: learning English, sharing knowledge about programming, technology, and creating content.

That's why I started this channel.

By talking about topics I'm passionate about, I can practice my English in a meaningful way while sharing ideas and experiences that may help other developers. I also believe that teaching is one of the best ways to learn. Every time I explain something, I understand it more deeply myself.

Beyond improving my English, this channel is also helping me become more confident, communicate better, and step outside of my comfort zone.

Most importantly, it's part of my effort to become a better version of myself—one video at a time.
