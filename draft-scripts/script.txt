## 🕒 Part 2 – Why English?

So, why did I choose English?

1. First, as a developer, English is Everywhere.
From coding to documentation, all are in English.
2. Second, the global tech community uses English.
Developers from different countries
communicate, learn, and help each other in English.
3. Third, English opens up better opportunities.
    
    With good English skills, you can access better job opportunities and earn higher salaries.
    
    Just take a look at job listings on LinkedIn. You'll notice that many positions require candidates to communicate confidently in English. The interview is often conducted in English, and in many international companies, your daily work, meetings, and collaboration with teammates are also in English.
    
    When you improve your English, you're not just learning a language—you're expanding your future.
    
4. And finally... Speaking English gives me more confidence.
    
    It helps me express my ideas more clearly, work with people from different countries, and become the kind of developer who can contribute anywhere in the world.
    
    That's the developer I want to become.
    

---

## 🕒 Part 3 – This Channel Is My Personal Practice

This channel is more than a place where I share programming knowledge or talk about life as a developer.

It's also my personal practice.

Over the years, I've tried many different ways to improve my English. I watched videos, I took courses, I used language learning apps. Some of them helped, but I still don't feel that my English has improved as much as I hoped.

Eventually, I realized that the only way to get better at English is through regular practice. Not for a few days or weeks, but continuously over a long period of time.

However, the one big challenge is, how to staying motivated. How do you practice every day without getting bored or giving up?

At the same time, I'd always wanted to start a YouTube channel. I just never found a topic that felt meaningful enough to keep going.

Then I asked myself: Why I’m not combine the things I love? Learning English, programming, technology, and creating content.

So I decided to start this channel.

Every video helps me practice speaking English while sharing ideas that might help other developers. And as they say, the best way to learn is to teach.

More than improving my English, this channel is helping me become a better communicator, build confidence, and step outside my comfort zone.

My goal isn't just to improve my English. It's to become a better version of myself—one video at a time.
